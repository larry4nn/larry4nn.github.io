# Xiaoyu Li

## Researcher at LongCat Team

I work on foundation models and agentic systems, with a focus on reliable reasoning, evaluation, post-training, and long-context intelligence.

My research studies how large language models acquire capabilities, how to evaluate them under challenging conditions, and how to improve their reliability in real-world tasks.

## Research

### Foundation Model Evaluation

Building benchmarks for reasoning, instruction following, long-context understanding, and agentic capabilities.

### Reasoning and Post-training

Improving model reasoning through reinforcement learning, test-time inference, self-correction, and capability-aware evaluation.

### Agentic Systems

Studying tool use, autonomous agents, trace-level behavior, and reliable interaction with complex environments.
