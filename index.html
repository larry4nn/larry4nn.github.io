Xiaoyu Li

I am a researcher on the Meituan LongCat Team, where I lead the Text Model Evaluation track—and previously led Multimodal Evaluation—within the Evaluation team.

As frontier models rapidly saturate static leaderboards, measurement becomes a first-order bottleneck to capability progress. My work focuses on building contamination-resistant, real-world evaluation frameworks for text foundation models—spanning full-cycle workplace agents (FORTE), Olympiad-grade mathematical reasoning (AMO-Bench), complex enterprise procedures (SOP-Maze), and million-token long-context profiling (ATLAS).

Earlier, I led large-scale human-aligned text-to-vision evaluation through Q-Eval-100K (CVPR 2025 Oral). I also contribute to evaluation-driven data quality scoring and filtering for agentic reasoning and tool use in the LongCat series.

FORTE: Full-Cycle Office Real-World Task Evaluation

Existing agent benchmarks rarely reflect end-to-end daily knowledge work across real enterprise artifacts. FORTE evaluates autonomous agents in containerized multimodal office workspaces (xlsx, docx, pptx, pdf, csv, images, and source code) across 15 corporate professions—from Finance, Legal, and Operations to Dev, SRE, and PM. Each task is graded under strict all-or-nothing expert rubrics, tracking both capability (Avg@3, Pass@3) and multi-run reliability (Pass^3).

180 tasks Full-cycle office work
15 professions Corporate domain coverage
Multimodal Containerized workspace
Avg@3 · Pass^3 All-or-nothing rubrics

AMO-Bench: Large Language Models Still Struggle in High School Math Competitions

As public competition suites such as AIME24/25 approach ceiling scores and suffer from data contamination, AMO-Bench introduces 50 entirely original, expert cross-validated mathematical reasoning problems at or above IMO difficulty, crafted for deterministic automatic final-answer grading. Evaluating 26 frontier LLMs shows even the strongest model achieves only 52.4% (with most scoring below 40%), revealing clear headroom and pronounced test-time compute scaling behavior.

Zero leakage Expert cross-validated

SOP-Maze: Evaluating Large Language Models on Complicated Business Standard Operating Procedures

Enterprise deployment demands reliable adherence to dense, branching Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) rather than isolated single-turn prompts. SOP-Maze adapts real-world business workflows into 397 instances and 3,422 subtasks across 23 complex SOP scenarios, structured into wide-option Lateral Root System (LRS) selection and deep-branch Heart Root System (HRS) logical execution—isolating three dominant failure modes: route blindness, conversational fragility, and contextual calculation errors.

397 instances Real business scenarios
3,422 subtasks Multi-step procedures
23 workflows Complex SOP graphs
LRS / HRS Breadth & depth taxonomy

04 / 04

Q-Eval-100K: Evaluating Visual Quality and Alignment Level for Text-to-Vision Content

Produced during my tenure leading Multimodal Evaluation in collaboration with SJTU, Q-Eval-100K builds the largest human-annotated benchmark for generative text-to-image and text-to-video models—100K instances (60K images and 40K videos) paired with 960K human Mean Opinion Scores covering visual quality and prompt alignment. Built on top of this dataset, Q-Eval-Score unifies quality and long-prompt alignment scoring in a single LMM evaluator with strong cross-benchmark generalization.

100K instances 60K images · 40K videos
960K MOS Human annotations
2 dimensions Quality & alignment
